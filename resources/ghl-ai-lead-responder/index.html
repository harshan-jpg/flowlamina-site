The guardrails that keep it out of trouble

An AI that talks to customers unsupervised has to know what it must never say. These rules are built into the agent, not left to chance.

🛑

Knows when to hand over

Billing, refunds, complaints, disputes or anything it isn't sure about get passed to a human — the AI never guesses on the things that could cost the business.

No costly mistakes
🙋

Humans always win

The moment a team member replies in a conversation, the AI steps back and stays quiet, so nobody ever gets two voices talking over each other.

One conversation, one voice
🤝

Never overpromises

No guarantees, no made-up prices, no pretending to be someone it isn't. It answers what it can and steers the rest to a call.

Says only what's true

Same owner, second business

The same pattern, pointed at bookings

The owner runs more than one business on the same platform. For his martial-arts gym I built a second agent with a different job: get every enquiry booked into a free trial class.

🥊 The gym agent

Answers questions about classes, works out the right program and age group, and books the lead into the correct class calendar on a real class day — the exact thing the previous attempt got wrong.

2 in 3 trial bookings in its first month were made by the AI, not a staff member

🧭 Why two agents, not one

The agency agent's job is a phone call. The gym agent's job is a calendar booking. Same platform, same guardrails, same always-on speed — but each one is built around the single action that makes that business money.
