Cooling service

What a cooling service actually covers

Ducted & reverse-cycle systems

•Full system inspection and performance assessment
•Filter clean, and replacement where it is needed
•Indoor and outdoor coil clean
•Refrigerant level check
•Electrical component inspection
•Thermostat and control system check
•Drain check and clean
•Fan and motor inspection, and a full safety inspection
 

Evaporative systems

Sitting under a cover all winter. The visit covers the water distribution check and clean, cooling pad inspection and replacement where required, pump service, reservoir clean, and the fan and motor.

Either way you get a written condition report after the visit, so you know what state the system is actually in rather than being told it is fine. All work is carried out in line with manufacturer recommendations and Australian standards, which protects any warranty still running on the system. See the full maintenance program.

Worth booking if any of these sound familiar

•It was struggling to keep up by the end of last summer
•It has not been serviced in the last couple of years
•It is making a noise it did not used to make
•It is over eight years old
•It is an evaporative system that has not been uncovered yet
 

Repair or replace

If it is getting on, get the numbers now

There is a point where another repair stops making sense, and it is worth knowing which side of that line a system sits on before summer rather than during it.

Say so when you call and we will give you an honest read on repair versus replacement, with a free quote either way. New installations carry a seven year workmanship warranty, and repair work is warranted for twelve months. We service and install all the major brands, including Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Panasonic, LG, Brivis, Braemar and Bonaire.

If a reverse-cycle upgrade is on the cards, it may attract a Victorian Energy Upgrades rebate. The office can check whether the property qualifies before you commit to anything.

Managing a property rather than living in it?

The maintenance program covers rental and investment properties, body corporate and strata, and commercial buildings — and the written condition report gives you something to put in front of an owner. If you look after several addresses, tell us and we will schedule them together.

Hot water is the other thing that only gets attention once it stops. We repair, service and replace hot water systems year round, so if one is old or has been playing up, it is easier dealt with now.
