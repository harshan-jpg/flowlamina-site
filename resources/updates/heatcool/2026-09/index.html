What's in it, and why

  • Every claim comes off your own website. The service inclusions — filters, coils, refrigerant, electricals, thermostat, drain, fan and motor — and the written condition report are taken word for word from your Scheduled Maintenance Program page. A customer who clicks through can verify all of it.
  • The written condition report is doing a lot of work. It is the difference between "we serviced it" and something the customer can hold, and it is the reason the property-manager section works.
  • Property managers get their own section. Your maintenance page already covers rental, investment, body corporate and strata — and a good share of your database is agencies looking after someone else's place, not homeowners.
  • A repair-or-replace nudge, without a hard sell. Free quote either way, the seven year workmanship warranty, and the Victorian Energy Upgrades rebate for a reverse-cycle upgrade.
  • No prices anywhere, and no promise about how fast you'll get there.

One thing I took out

The gutters half of September

Your plan pairs cooling service with "Are your gutters ready for spring rain?", and I agree with the timing. But there is no plumbing, roofing, gutter or stormwater page anywhere on heatcoolmasters.com.au — the site lists heating, air conditioning, hot water and commercial only.

Sending 20,000 customers to a website that cannot confirm you do the work costs more than the campaign earns, and it is the interested ones who click. So the gutter copy is written and waiting, and goes in the month a page for it goes up. That is a quick fix, and it unlocks September, October and November.

Three quick things I need from you

  1. The wording — happy with it, or anything you want changed? It is your voice going to your customers, so say if a line does not sound like you.
  2. The HCM20 discount is deliberately not in this one. It is the offer on the reactivation texts, and putting it in a newsletter as well means customers who were never in that group start asking for it. Tell me if you would rather it went in.
  3. Is the ARC licence number AU16233 still current? I could not find it anywhere on the site, so I have left it out for now.
