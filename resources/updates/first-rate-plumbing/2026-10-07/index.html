Job checklist

Every job, every tech, the same way.

If it isn't in ServiceM8, it didn't happen. And it can't be invoiced.

1Before you leave

  • Open the job. Read the description and the Job Diary, and check the address, access notes and contact number.
  • Tap On My Way so the customer gets a text with your arrival time.

2When you arrive

  • Check In to the job. This starts your time on it.
  • Take before photos in the job.

3On the job

  • If the job has a checklist, tick it off as you go.
  • Add every part to Materials as you fit it, not at the end of the day.
  • Picking up parts for this job? Give the counter the job number, and add a photo of the docket to the job.

4Before you drive off

  • Take after photos.
  • Write a note in the Job Diary: what you did, what you found, and what's still needed. Write it so the office can invoice without ringing you.
  • Get a signature if the job needs one.
  • Check Out of the job.
  • Finished? Set the job to Completed. Coming back? Leave it as Work Order and add a note saying why: parts, a second visit, or access.
  • If the office has asked you to take payment, take it in the app against the invoice.

5End of day, 2 minutes

  • Go through today's jobs. Each one is either Completed or has a "coming back because…" note.
  • Each one has its materials (or a note saying "no materials") and its photos.
