Every sale started with a manual quote

For a technical manufacturer, every deal begins the same way: a customer emails to ask what a product costs for a given quantity. Answering means a person reading the request — sometimes a scanned PDF or an RFQ — finding the product, looking up the right price for that quantity, and writing a formal quote. By hand, one at a time.

It's accurate, but it's slow, and it ties up skilled people in repetitive work. The brief was clear: keep the simple "just email us" promise customers rely on — but do the heavy lifting automatically, inside the Zoho CRM the business already runs on.

How the automation works

From an inbound email to a reviewable quote — end to end, inside Zoho CRM.

  1. Read every incoming email

    It watches the shared sales inbox and reads the whole request — the subject, the body, and any attachments, including PDFs, RFQs, spreadsheets and scanned documents. Quote details hide in all three, so all three are read.

  2. Understand & classify it

    An AI model works out whether it's a quote request, detects the language it's written in, and extracts the exact products and quantities being asked for.

  3. Price it from Zoho's price books

    It matches the sender to a Contact in Zoho CRM, then for each product reads Zoho's tiered Price Books and picks the correct per-unit price for the requested quantity. Pricing comes only from Zoho — never invented or estimated.

  4. Draft the quote in Zoho CRM

    It generates a professional, on-brand quote in the customer's language and creates a real Draft Quote record in Zoho — with a structured line-item table — plus a ready-to-send draft email saved right under the quote.

  5. Review, send — and nothing slips

    A person opens the draft in Zoho, checks it, and clicks send — the automation never sends anything itself. And if a product or customer can't be matched confidently, it opens a Manual Review task in Zoho instead of guessing, so every request is accounted for.

Built deep into Zoho CRM

This isn't a bolt-on that keeps its own copy of the data — it reads and writes Zoho's real modules through the Zoho CRM API.

Single source of truth

Contacts, Products, Price Books and Quotes all live in Zoho. The automation reads and writes them natively — Zoho stays the one system of record.

Tiered price-book logic

It reads per-product, per-quantity list prices from Zoho's Price Books and matches the right tier to the order size — the pricing rules the business already uses, applied automatically.

Native Quote records

It creates genuine Zoho Quote records at Draft stage with a structured line-item subform — not a PDF stapled on the side of the CRM.

Draft email under the quote

The customer-ready email is saved as a draft on the Quote itself, so staff send from Zoho exactly as they always have — no new tool to learn.

Accurate by design

A quote that goes to a customer has to be right — so the guardrails come first.

Accuracy & control

  • Human-in-the-loop — every quote is reviewed before it's sent
  • No auto-send, ever — the automation only drafts
  • Prices come only from Zoho's price books
  • The AI is forbidden from inventing or adjusting a number

Fits how they already work

  • Bilingual — replies in the sender's language
  • Keeps the "just email us" promise unchanged
  • Staff keep sending from Zoho, as before
  • Unmatched requests become review tasks — never wrong quotes
