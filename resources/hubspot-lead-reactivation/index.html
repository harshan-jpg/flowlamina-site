Case study · HubSpot CRM

Turning a CRM full of dormant leads back into booked calls

A self-running reactivation engine, built natively inside a client's HubSpot CRM. It rebuilds a targetable list from years of gone-quiet contacts, runs a personalised SMS-and-email sequence, and hands every reply to an AI agent that qualifies the lead and books the call.

HubSpot CRM AI SMS + email agent ~5,600 dormant deals Details sanitised — no client data

The buried asset every business is sitting on

Established businesses all share the same untapped asset: thousands of contacts who once sampled, enquired or interacted — then went quiet. They already know the brand, which makes them the cheapest revenue in the business to win.

But no one has the hours to work back through thousands of them by hand. So they sit in the CRM's cold pipeline, untouched, month after month. This engine works that list automatically — persistently, personally, and safely.

How the engine works

From a cold contact to a booked call — end to end, inside HubSpot.

  1. Build the target list

    No clean "dormant" list exists, so the engine assembles one from signals — past interaction, gone quiet, not yet a customer. Messy phone data is cleaned and normalised, and contacts split into two cohorts: win-back (lapsed customers) and re-intro (sampled, never bought).

  2. Reach out — multi-touch

    Five touches over roughly two weeks, alternating SMS and email, personalised per contact. SMS leads where a valid mobile exists; email carries the rest. The sequence stops the instant someone replies.

  3. AI takes the conversation

    A reply hands the thread to an AI agent. It qualifies conversationally, answers only from an approved knowledge base, and steers toward booking — capturing the day, time and contact details. It never discounts, never makes unverified claims, and never pretends to be human.

  4. Book & hand off

    The agent books the call into a staff calendar and progresses the HubSpot deal stage. Anything sensitive — complaints, edge cases, anyone asking for a person — routes cleanly to a single human.

  5. Everything on the record

    Every touch, reply and booking is written back to the HubSpot contact timeline, so the CRM stays the single source of truth. Nothing lives in a black box off to the side.

Built to run safely

Messaging a large dormant database is only worth doing if it's done properly.

Guardrails & compliance

  • A defensible consent basis before the first send
  • Working opt-out, honoured and mirrored back into the CRM
  • Safe sending hours for the contact's timezone
  • A hard "never" list the AI agent cannot cross

State & two-way sync

  • Per-contact state: touch count, last touched, replied, booked, opted-out
  • Held in HubSpot, with a scheduler that decides who's due next
  • Stops instantly on any reply, on any channel
  • HubSpot stays the single source of truth
