Case study · HubSpot CRM

4 sales call follow‑ups a day from a dead database

A food manufacturer's HubSpot had years of build-up: venues that sampled, enquired or ordered once, then went quiet. Nobody had the hours to work back through them. I built a reactivation engine natively inside their own HubSpot that does it by itself. It texts 80 new contacts every day, answers every reply, and hands the warm ones straight to their sales team. The result is around four sales call follow-ups a day, from people who had stopped talking to them.

These people already know the brand. They are the cheapest revenue in the business — and they were sitting there untouched.

The simple idea

Every established business is sitting on the same buried asset: thousands of people who already sampled, enquired or bought — then drifted. Winning one of them back costs a fraction of finding someone new.

📦

1. They already knew you

They took a sample, asked for a price, or ordered once. Then life got busy on both sides and the conversation stopped.

→
📱

2. Eighty a day, quietly

The engine picks the next eighty off the list each day and texts them. Never a blast — a steady trickle nobody has to run.

→
🤝

3. Warm ones go to a human

An AI handles the back-and-forth. The moment someone's genuinely interested, it hands them to the salesperson with the full conversation attached.

Not a design — it's running

Started at twenty a day. Now eighty.

The engine went live on a real list, not a test one, at twenty new contacts a day to prove it was safe. Once it was, the dial went up to eighty. That is where the sales call follow-ups come from.

The first days, at twenty a day

What the AI actually does

It isn't there to close. It's there to find out whether there's anything real here, and if there is, get a human involved fast.

The handover is the product

A warm lead lands in HubSpot, not in a chat log

Plenty of AI tools have good conversations and then drop them. This one's job is to make sure a person picks the lead up — exactly once, with everything they need.

📊

The deal moves itself

The HubSpot deal is renamed with the venue and why they're interested, and moved to the right stage. Nobody drags a card.

The pipeline stays honest
✅

One task, on one person

A high-priority task appears on the salesperson's HubSpot list with the full conversation attached, so they never open cold.

No lead falls between people
📧

One email, ever

If the same lead gets flagged again, the system reuses the deal and updates the task — it does not send a second alert. Alert fatigue kills handovers.

The alerts stay worth reading

Messaging thousands of people is only worth doing properly

Built to run safely

The list is a real asset. Burning it with a badly-run campaign costs more than the campaign could ever make.

🚫

Opting out is silent and final

A STOP, or a plain "take me off the list", removes them everywhere — with no confirmation message and no last try. Three in every hundred took it; the rest stayed.

Consent handled properly
⏰

A reply stops the sequence dead

Nobody ever receives a scheduled marketing text while they're mid-conversation. Only contacts in the active sequence can be touched at all.

It never talks over itself
📜

HubSpot stays the source of truth

Every touch, reply, opt-out, task and booking is written back to the contact timeline. Nothing lives in a black box off to the side.

You own the record

Of roughly 5,600 cold deals, only 788 were genuinely targetable once duplicates, bad numbers, existing customers and prior opt-outs were stripped out. The other 854 that looked usable were set aside on purpose. Cleaning the list first is most of the work, and skipping it is how businesses get their sending account shut down.
