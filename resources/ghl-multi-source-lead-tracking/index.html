Case study · GoHighLevel forms & lead tracking

Know exactly where every lead comes from

This business gets customers three ways — partner referrals, Meta ads, and its website. I set up forms inside GoHighLevel that capture every lead, tag it with the source it really came from, credit partner referrals to the right partner automatically, and feed them all into one clean pipeline — and into each partner's own portal.

No more guessing which ads work, which partners deliver, or who to thank for a sale.

The simple idea

Leads arrive from all directions. The job is to know — for every single one — where it came from, and who deserves the credit.

📥

1. A lead arrives

Someone fills in a form — sent by a partner, from a Meta ad, or on the website.

→
🏷️

2. The form tags it

It's stamped with its true source — and if a partner sent them, that partner is credited for good.

→
🔗

3. It syncs everywhere

The lead lands in one clean pipeline — and shows up live in the right partner's portal.

One form for every source

Each way a lead can arrive has its own form — and each form quietly stamps the lead with where it came from, so nothing lands as a mystery.

Every referral credited to the right partner — for good

The partner who first sends a customer is locked in as the source. Even if that customer comes back weeks later through an ad, the original partner keeps the credit — so no one loses a sale they earned.

Because the first touch is locked, the same person arriving twice never becomes two leads, and a later ad click can't quietly steal a partner's credit. One honest record, one rightful owner.

It flows straight into the partner portal

The moment a partner's referral is captured and credited, it appears in that partner's own live portal — so they can watch their leads without anyone sending a spreadsheet.
