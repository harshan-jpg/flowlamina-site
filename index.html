Frequently asked questions

Safety and data sovereignty

Who owns the data and the systems you build?

You do. Everything we build lives in your own accounts: your CRM, your automation platform, your hosting and your AI provider. Your data, workflows and code sit in accounts you own and pay for directly, never in ours, so nothing about your business is locked inside Flowlamina.

What happens if we stop working together?

Everything keeps running. Because the whole system sits in your accounts, nothing switches off when an engagement ends. You get a handover handbook that explains how to run it, change it and turn it off, so your team or any other developer can look after it without us.

Where is my data hosted?

In the region that best suits your business and your obligations. For Australian businesses we use Australian data centres wherever the platform offers them; for clients overseas we choose the region closest to them or the one their industry requires. Before anything goes live we tell you plainly where each part of the system stores data.

How do you keep access to our systems safe?

We work through logins and keys that you create and can revoke at any time, with only the access the job needs. Credentials are kept in a password vault, never in code, email or chat, and our access is removed at handover. AI runs through your own business account with the provider, on plans where your data is not used to train their models by default.
